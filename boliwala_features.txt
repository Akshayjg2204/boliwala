1. Product Overview
Boliwala.com is India's first dedicated platform for SARFAESI bank auction properties — properties seized by banks from loan defaulters and sold through public e-auctions. An estimated ₹3.5 lakh crore of bank auction properties go unsold annually because there is no single platform where buyers can discover, evaluate, and bid on them.
Boliwala solves this with three things: free, transparent property listings (including the full address — unlike competitors who paywall it), a paid service that manages the entire auction process for serious buyers, and a channel partner program where brokers earn commissions on all revenue streams.

4. Channel Partner Portal
Channel partners (brokers/agents) have their own login where they build a referral network, earn commissions on three revenue streams, and access co-branded marketing creatives.
4.1  Channel Partner Signup & Onboarding
Partners visit /channel-partner-signup
Enter: name, email, phone, business name (optional)
Email verification required
First login shows onboarding: commission structure, copy referral link, invite contacts
4.2  Dashboard & Earnings
Summary cards: Total Referrals, Active Users, Purchased (₹999), Purchased (₹9,999), Auction Wins
Earnings breakdown:
₹999 subscriptions commission
₹9,999 packages commission
Success fees commission (% of 1%)
Total earnings this month
Filterable referral table: Name, Email, Status, Package, Earnings, Date joined
Commission rates display (read-only, set by admin)
4.3  Referral Link & Unlimited Invites
Unique referral link with copy button: boliwala.com/join?ref=CP_abc123xyz

Partner name

₹999 subscription — configured % per sub
₹9,999 per-auction package — configured % per package

6. Marketing & User Engagement
6.1  Property Match Alerts
Real-time trigger on new/edited listings against all active subscriptions
Email instant; WhatsApp Phase 1 (manual), Phase 2 (Business API)
Also: auction reminders, shortlist changes, inspection day reminders
6.2  Email Lifecycle (10 templates)
Welcome (3 parts), Weekly Digest, Re-engagement (3 milestones), Post-Callback, Post-Auction, Abandoned Alert
6.3  Credit Lifecycle (free → ₹999)
2 credits left → soft nudge
Last credit used → paywall alert
After 3 days unpaid → social proof nudge
6.4  Subscription Lifecycle (₹999/year)
Purchase → receipt + renewal date
Renewal reminders at 30, 7 days, and on expiry
Win-back 14 days after lapse
6.5  Cross-Sell (₹999 → ₹9,999)
On 3+ unlocks or heavy shortlisting: "Found one serious? Let experts handle — ₹9,999, 1% only if you win"
6.6  WhatsApp Strategy
Phase 1: click-to-chat links, manual alert queue, export segments
Phase 2: Business API, automated alerts, two-way replies to Callback queue
6.7  Admin Engagement Tools
Alert Engine, Email Campaigns, WhatsApp Tools, Segments & Export, Analytics

8. Key Requirements
Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)
SEO optimized with server-side rendering
Indian formatting (₹, lakh/crore grouping)
No hardcoded prices or credit values — all adjustable from admin
Auth: browse/search/address/callback = no login; shortlist/unlock/purchase = login required
View counts are real server-tracked counters
Alerts match in real-time, not nightly batch
Bulk Excel upload with row preview before commit
Channel partner referral links are unique and tracked; commissions automatic
Commission rates adjustable by admin, globally applied, audit-logged
Marketing creatives auto-personalized per partner; admin uploads templates once
₹9,999 package is per-auction (one property); users buy separate packages for multiple properties
Admin can upload unlimited documents per package; users view in profile
