Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I pay the 1% success fee? +
The 1% success fee is charged only if you actually win the auction. If you don't win, you pay nothing beyond the ₹9,999 base fee. This aligns our incentives with yours.
Is browsing properties really free? +
Yes. All property listings, auction details, reserve prices, and EMD amounts are free to view. You only pay if you choose to hire us for the end-to-end service. Sign up free to also unlock inspection dates and bank officer contacts.
What does due diligence include? +
Legal title verification, physical possession status, outstanding dues check, regulatory approvals, independent valuation, and the post-auction sale certificate process — delivered by city-specific empanelled lawyers and valuers.
Which banks and portals do you cover? +
We cover all major banks including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Bank of Baroda, PNB and more, sourcing from government-mandated portals like baanknet.com (IBAPI) and MSTC, plus newspaper notices and individual bank websites.
